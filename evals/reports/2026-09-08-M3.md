  id. The same skill, the same task, opposite provenance, all five graded pass,
  and no S2 check looks at author, raised-by or model (F-058).

  beside it, and three of its five seals are weaker than claimed (F-059).
- **E6 has no gate at all**: nothing in the runner, `run.sh` or CI reads
  `e6-results.json` (F-060).

Neither fact is established by the gate that graded it. Everything below stands
as a record of what was built and measured; read it with this section in force,
and in particular read the "proved, not asserted" section as proving five
specific tamper cases and not the gate as a whole.
