# Sign-offs

Append only, and never edited. A sign-off is the state transition: it is what
locks a decision or meets a criterion. Deleting or rewriting one is the single
change this schema treats as tampering.

Entry shape, copy it exactly:

    ### S-001
    actor: your-name
    role: reviewer
    date: 2026-01-01
    scope: [D-001, AC-001]

    What you are signing off, and on what evidence.
